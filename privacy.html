1. Introduction

At Winny's Christian Zone ("we", "us", or "our"), accessible via https://winnyschristianzone.com, we hold the privacy of our customers and visitors in the highest regard. This Privacy Policy details how personal information is collected, processed, and safeguarded in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), and Meta Platform standards.

2. Information We Collect

When you visit our online store or place a purchase, we collect the necessary details to process your blessed items smoothly:

  • Customer Identity & Contact: Full Name, Email Address, Contact Phone Number, and Delivery Shipping Address.
  • Gift & Recipient Personalization Data: If you select our gift order feature, we collect the Gift Recipient's Name, Recipient WhatsApp Number, and custom prayer notes provided by you.
  • Transaction Data: Razorpay Payment ID, Order Reference Number, and payment status. Note: We never process, store, or have access to your full credit/debit card numbers, CVV, or banking passwords. All payments are encrypted end-to-end via Razorpay (PCI-DSS Level 1 certified gateway).
  • Device & Browsing Analytics: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and timestamp data for fraud detection and performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly for legitimate commercial and operational purposes, including:

  • Processing, verifying, and fulfilling your orders and shipping merchandise to your doorstep.
  • Dispatching order confirmations, digital receipts, and tracking updates via Email and WhatsApp Business API.
  • Delivering personal gift notifications and prayer blessings to recipients specified by the buyer.
  • Providing responsive concierge customer support and resolving returns or inquiries.
  • Complying with applicable Indian taxation laws, GST reporting, and statutory guidelines.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We share minimal required data solely with audited, trusted third-party partners essential for our operations:

  • Payment Gateways: Razorpay Software Private Limited (for encrypted payment authorization).
  • Database & Cloud Hosting: Supabase Inc. (PostgreSQL cloud architecture with Row-Level Security) and GitHub Pages.
  • Messaging & Communication: Meta Platforms Inc. (WhatsApp Business Cloud API) for automated dispatch alerts.
  • Logistics & Couriers: Partnered Indian courier services (India Post, BlueDart, Delhivery) for physical parcel fulfillment.

5. Data Security & Storage

All sensitive communications are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / SSL 256-bit). Private credentials and payment signing keys are stored exclusively in isolated database vaults with strict access permissions.
